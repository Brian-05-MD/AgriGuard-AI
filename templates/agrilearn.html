Soil Moisture

Soil moisture shows the amount of water available in the soil for plant growth. Very low soil moisture can cause water stress.

Rainfall

Rainfall provides natural water for crops. Low rainfall combined with low soil moisture may require additional water management.

Common maize pests can damage leaves, stems and developing crops. Regular inspection helps farmers identify pest problems early.

Crop Diseases

Crop diseases may cause spots, discoloration, wilting or abnormal plant growth. Early identification can reduce crop losses.

Irrigation

Irrigation provides water to crops when rainfall is insufficient. Water should be applied according to crop needs and soil conditions.
